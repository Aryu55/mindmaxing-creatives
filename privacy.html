Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 1, 2026

1. Overview

At Mindmaxing Creatives, we respect your privacy. We build software tools and custom projects. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to spammers, data brokers, or third-party advertisers.

2. Information We Collect

When you fill out our "Start a project" contact form, we collect:

  • Contact Details: Your Name, Email Address, and optional Phone / WhatsApp number.
  • Project Information: Your selected budget range and project description.

This information is used strictly to respond to your inquiry and scope your project.

3. How We Process Data

Form submissions are processed via Cloudflare Pages Functions and securely dispatched through the Resend API directly to our internal email inbox. We do not store your lead information in public databases or share it with third parties.

4. Cookies & Analytics

This website uses zero invasive tracking scripts or third-party advertising pixels. Minimal technical cookies may be used strictly for performance and essential layout functionality.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding your data, email us directly at mindmaxxxing@gmail.com.
