## 1. The Core Strategic Invariants (Strict Rules)

Every piece of copy generated under this skill must adhere to these 8 invariants without exception:

### Invariant 1: "I Made This" Always Beats "Want Me to Make This?"
Never ask prospects for permission to work. Walk in with the proof, the teardown, or the pre-built asset already complete.
> *"now the part that doubles replies: don't offer to help. help then offer. lemme explain... 'i made this' beats 'want me to make this?' every time."*

### Invariant 2: The Two-Trigger Rule (Momentum + Problem)
Reaching out to a struggling business feels predatory and accusatory. Reaching out to a growing business with an acute operational bottleneck gets immediate replies.
> *"the best leads have 2 triggers at once. one good thing + one bad thing in the same outreach email.*  
> *example:*  
> *> a clinic opens a second location + gets 3 bad reviews there.*  
> *> a brand launches a product + stops running ads.*  
> *momentum + a problem = they reply."*

### Invariant 3: Timing and Relevance Beat "Clever" Copy
Nobody cares about clever linguistic hooks, jokes, or poetic wordplay. Prospects reply when you catch them at the exact moment their workflow broke, holding a pre-made fix.
> *"we tested 1,000+ cold emails. the ones that got replies didn't have better copy. they were sent at the right moment, with something already made."*

### Invariant 4: Scannable 4th-Grade Copy (50 to 80 Words Max)
- Write like a peer founder typing on an iPhone between meetings.
- 1 sentence per paragraph with generous white space.
- Plain street-level English at a 4th-grade reading level (Liz Wilcox style).
- Strictly ban corporate consultant buzzwords ("form fatigue", "enterprise clutter", "pipeline architecture", "synergy", "cutting-edge", "game-changer", "I hope this finds you well").

### Invariant 5: The Soft Frictionless CTA (Curiosity Question)
Never ask for a 30-minute demo, discovery call, or calendar link on a cold message. Ask a simple, low-commitment curiosity question:
- *"Mind if I drop the 20-second video clip here?"*
- *"Would you like to have a look at it?"*
- *"Worth sending the quick teardown over?"*

### Invariant 6: The Strict Zero Em-Dash Rule (Universal Formatting Invariant)
Never use em dashes anywhere in generated outreach copy, subject lines, follow-ups, or explanations. Replace them with standard commas, periods, colons, parentheses, or simple hyphens (` - `). Em dashes project synthetic AI generation and fail human copy standards.

### Invariant 7: Outbound Sales Call Inversion (No Discovery Quizzing)
Stop asking 10 discovery questions on sales calls booked through outbound. The prospect did not book inbound to be interrogated. Walk into the call presenting:
1. The Diagnosis (where visitors/revenue are dropping)
2. The Forensic Proof (live devtools or 20-second clip)
3. The Roadmap & Fix (how the resolved experience functions)
4. The Bounded Sprint Offer ($500 to $1,000 fixed price)

### Invariant 8: The Public Embarrassment Wedge (The "Ugly Email" Rule)
Never be afraid of raw, unpolished plain-text emails. Pick one tiny, unaddressed detail that publicly embarrasses the brand (Reddit threads, 1-star reviews, AI search citations, or mobile bugs) and tie it directly to revenue bleed. A raw, authentic note pointing out a public bruise converts 9x better than a sanitized corporate pitch.

## 4. Creative Pattern Interrupts (High-Effort Formats for Top 50-100 Accounts)

When sending outreach to tier-1 enterprise or high-ticket accounts, generic plain text can blend into the noise. Deploy Suprava's creative pattern interrupts:

### Format 1: The Handwritten Letter DM
1. **Lookalike Sourcing:** Paste your best client domains into lookalike tools (e.g. Ocean.io) to find 100 identical companies.
2. **Psychological Trigger Mapping:** Extract recent founder posts, podcasts, or job openings that have been vacant for 3+ months.
3. **Handwritten Letter Generation:** Draft a concise note stating their exact operational pain point paired with your sprint offer. Render it into a realistic handwritten letter graphic.
4. **Remove AI Artifacts:** Inspect manually to ensure authentic, human pen-on-paper presentation.
5. **Direct Dispatch:** Send the image via LinkedIn DM to 1st connections or key decision-makers.

### Format 2: The Personalized Industry Meme
- Craft a custom meme where the visual joke itself demonstrates that you intimately understand their exact technical or operational bottleneck.
- If the prospect laughs and agrees with the joke, you instantly prove domain authority without writing a 300-word pitch.

### Format 3: The Mini Slide Deck (PPT)
- When an idea, CRO teardown, or architectural blueprint would take 500 words of dense text to explain, condense it into 3 to 5 visual slides.
- Export as images or a lightweight PDF preview.

### Format 4: The 20-Second Mobile Teardown (Loom / Screen Capture)
- Record the exact storefront component failing live on iOS Safari or Android mobile (e.g., cart drawer freezing, quantity reset, layout shift).
- Seeing the glitch live converts 5x higher than describing it in text.

## 5. The Upfront Pricing Reply Protocol (The DM Handler)

When a prospect replies to cold outreach or DMs asking *"How much do you usually charge?"* before revealing project details:

### The 3 Rules:
1. **Never quote a flat low price:** Flat quotes like ₹15,000 or $200 anchor you as a cheap commodity and trigger solo burnout on scope creep.
2. **Never dodge the question:** Grilling them with 5 discovery questions without addressing price feels defensive and gets ghosted.
3. **Use Bracket Pricing + Scope Bifurcation:** Anchor at $500 to $1,200 (or ₹40,000 to ₹95,000), explain the scope difference, and conclude with a single 1-sentence diagnostic question.

### Ready-to-Send Reply Templates:

#### Option A: USD Global Anchor (Reddit, X, International Leads)
```text
Usually between $500 to $1,200 depending on the scope.

A focused, high-converting single product store sits on the lower end, while a full custom multi-category catalog with custom flows sits closer to $1,000+.

To give you an exact number: are you launching a brand new product from scratch, or do you already have an existing store and products running?
```

#### Option B: Dual Currency Anchor (Indian D2C, WhatsApp Leads)
```text
Usually between $500 to $1,200 (around ₹40,000 to ₹95,000) depending on the scope.

A clean, high-converting launch for 1-3 products sits on the lower end, while a full multi-category store with custom checkout sits toward the higher end.

To give you an exact figure: are you launching a brand new product from scratch, or do you already have an existing store and inventory running?
```

### Next-Step Branch Logic:
- **If they answer "Existing store / live brand":** Ask for their URL (`"Drop the link here, I'll take a quick look at the mobile flow"`). Run a 2-minute mobile audit, spot a broken drawer or friction point, and send a 20-second clip showing the fix. Close a $500 to $1,000 sprint.
- **If they answer "Brand new / zero budget / dropshipping idea":** Offer a bounded, no-custom-code template setup at a firm floor ($500 / ₹40,000). If their budget is under ₹10,000, politely disqualify them.

## 6. The 0 to 100 Customers GTM & Signal Stacking Playbook

From Suprava's viral GTM masterclass (Tweet #58 & #60):

1. **Signal Stacking Over Volume:**
   - Never target generic lists like "SaaS + CMO".
   - Stack multiple micro-signals: *"SaaS + new CMO + hiring demand gen + raised funding recently"*.
2. **Job Posts as Pain Indicators:**
   - A job post is an urgent cry for help. Read the description to extract the exact operational headaches the company is trying to solve right now, then pitch the sprint fix directly to the executive.
3. **7+ Segments Before Adding Volume:**
   - Segment outreach by the trigger event that creates buying intent:
     - New CMO hired
     - CMO hiring demand gen
     - CMO launching new product
     - CMO replacing a competitor
4. **Agency Partnerships as a Distribution Layer:**
   - Partner with established agencies that already manage 20 to 100 clients in your niche.
   - Package your technical sprint into their retainer offering so they resell your service to improve their own margins.
5. **No Freemium Strategy Early On:**
   - Freemium attracts broke, non-paying users. Offer a strong paid offer with a bounded 7-day trial or paid pilot from day one.

## 7. Quality & Invariant Verification Checklist

Before finalizing any cold email or DM sequence, run this checklist:

- [ ] **Word Count Check:** Is the body strictly between 40 and 80 words?
- [ ] **Subject Line Check:** Is the subject line 2 to 4 words, lowercase, conversational, and free of emojis/hype?
- [ ] **Two-Trigger Pairing:** Does the message include both a positive momentum anchor and a forensic problem?
- [ ] **Scannability Check:** Is every paragraph exactly 1 sentence with clean line breaks?
- [ ] **CTA Friction Check:** Is the call-to-action a soft curiosity question rather than a request for a 30-minute call?
- [ ] **Strict Zero Em-Dash Check:** Are there zero em dashes in the subject line, body, or signature?
- [ ] **Jargon Ban Check:** Are all corporate consultant buzzwords removed in favor of 4th-grade plain English?
