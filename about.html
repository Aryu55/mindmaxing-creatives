Mindmaxing is an independent creative engineering and motion design studio led by Aryan Panchal and Rahul Saranya. We work where digital product, storefront engineering and commercial visual direction meet.

Good taste is not a handoff.

It belongs in the decisions: what the interface asks of someone, how the system behaves under pressure, what should be removed, and where a little strangeness makes the experience more memorable. We keep those questions attached to the build from the first sketch through the final handoff.

The studio leadership.

Aryan Panchal

Founder · Systems, Creative Engineering & Storefront Architecture

Architects bespoke storefront systems, headless commerce engines, and high-concurrency software products. 14 live commercial systems and web engines shipped.

Rahul Saranya

Partner · 3D Animation, Motion Graphics & Visual Direction

Directs commercial 3D animation, motion graphics, and visual design. Commercial portfolio includes animation campaigns across Fevicol, Britannia TVC, Fiama, Cuticura, and MotoGP.

No relay race.

You speak directly with the people shaping and building the work. We agree the edges before production, share the decisions as they happen, and leave behind a result the next person can understand.
