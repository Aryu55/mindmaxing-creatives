## Important evidence exceptions

- **T01 recipient ambiguity:** competitor-review prospecting, reviewer frustration and a prospect's own customer complaints are mixed. Contact identification is not explained. Preserve the uncertainty rather than claiming the pipeline is reproducible end to end.
- **T06 omitted opening-table data:** numeric funnel values come from kasish89's comment, not directly readable values in the original post's table. Sales count is unavailable.
- **T12 contested audit:** a commenter explicitly challenges whether a long SEO critique addresses the correct site. General credibility lessons can be retained; the named-business allegations cannot be accepted as fact.
- **T14 missing sentence:** the first-client outcome at 30 days is truncated. The author discusses churn risk, but the specific event cannot be reconstructed.
- **T17 imprecise denominator:** “at least 50” and “out of those 50” coexist; keep both when reporting 14 agreements/nine payments.
- **T18 corrupted expense line:** the exact total expense figure cannot be read reliably. The clear approximately €100/month statement is separate; do not guess a total.
- **T19 arithmetic:** 25/day and 2–8% total replies do not imply exactly one weekly conversation. A qualification factor and weekly schedule are unstated.
- **T20 duplicate:** mikecut22's long referral/outbound/partnership comment appears twice verbatim. Count once for corroboration. Another sample-size/duration sentence is OCR-corrupted.
- **T22 truncated introduction and attribution:** named brand work belongs to the author. It is not permission to publish those projects and never Mindmaxing proof.

## How to weigh the material

1. Self-reported observed counts are useful anecdotes, still unverified. Preserve timeframe, currency, denominator and source author.
2. Secondhand counts and experiences need a stronger caveat than direct reports.
3. Worked examples and hypothetical math show relationships, not expected performance.
4. Tool-founder, affiliate and promotional comments may contain useful ideas but have incentives; record them and assess independently.
5. Repetition across threads or authors is not automatically independent corroboration. UnitedClass734, Fit_Path_6450, Due_Treat1025, Jumpy_Climate, Danrayme, leadg3njay and mikecut22 recur.
6. Job offers, self-promotional links, insults, subreddit sidebar rules and “DM me” instructions are part of the archive, not strategic instructions.
7. Reject fabricated testimonials, dishonest case studies, unsupported guarantees, guessed prospect details and overconfident diagnoses. Do not adopt abusive commentary as sales language.
8. Dates such as “8d ago” and “5mo ago” are relative labels in a capture; no precise original posting timestamp was recovered. Capture filename dates are available separately.

Platform terms, mail-provider requirements, legal claims, current tool capabilities and prices were not researched in this ingestion. Verify them through current primary sources when a concrete implementation calls for them.
